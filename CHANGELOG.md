### Changed
- Upgrade pvbt dependency to v0.7.7

## [0.1.0] - 2026-04-21

### Added
- Initial release of Accelerating Dual Momentum strategy
- Monthly momentum scoring using 1-, 3-, and 6-month lookback periods (Momentum631) to select between risk-on and risk-off assets
- Risk-adjusted momentum scoring via pvbt's RiskAdjustedPct, with full justification output for each rebalance
